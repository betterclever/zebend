Verify Zcash shielded proofs in your browser

These are real Zcash mainnet transactions. Each Orchard/Ironwood bundle carries a Halo 2 zero-knowledge proof. This page checks it with a verifier written in Bend, compiled to JavaScript, running entirely on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Verifier: Bend 2, generated from the Orchard verifying key, agrees with the official halo2_proofs verifier on every mainnet bundle we tested. Post-NU6.3 circuit. Single-threaded; a 2-action proof takes about 10–15 s.
